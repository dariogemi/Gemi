CASE STUDY · BUSINESS → TECHNOLOGY

Turning “we need this” into something a technology team can actually build.

A practical requirements-engineering path from an ambiguous business request to a shared understanding, delivery-ready scope and testable outcome.

DiscoveryWorkflowsSpecificationsAcceptance criteriaUAT

THE CHALLENGE

Ambiguity is expensive when it reaches development.

Business stakeholders often describe outcomes, while technology teams need behaviour, rules, dependencies, data and exceptions. The gap between those languages creates rework.

The BA creates the bridge: clarify the objective, model the process, expose edge cases, document the required behaviour and make the expected result testable.

FROM INTENT TO DELIVERY

01

Discover

Clarify the business objective, users, actors, constraints and success criteria.

02

Model

Translate the conversation into workflows, rules, scenarios, dependencies and system interactions.

03

Specify

Produce functional behaviour, interfaces, data expectations, exceptions and acceptance criteria.

04

Validate

Walk the specification with stakeholders and delivery teams, then support UAT and defect clarification.

THE VALUE

The deliverable is not the document. It is shared understanding.

A good specification reduces interpretation risk. It gives business stakeholders confidence that their intent is represented and gives engineers, testers and operators a concrete model of what needs to happen.
