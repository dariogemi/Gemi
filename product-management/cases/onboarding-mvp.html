Designing an onboarding MVP around customer value, not feature volume.

A product-thinking case focused on reducing friction in a financial onboarding journey while balancing regulatory, operational and technical constraints.

THE PRODUCT QUESTION

Where is the journey losing customers?

For a financial onboarding product, adding more screens and more checks does not automatically create a better product. The first task is understanding where customers abandon, where operations intervene, and which constraints are genuinely non-negotiable.

The product approach starts with the journey and the outcome, then works backward into capabilities and delivery increments.

MVP LOGIC

01

Discover

Map the journey, user needs, business goals, operational friction and regulatory constraints.

02

Define value

Identify the smallest experience that improves a meaningful customer and business outcome.

03

Prioritise

Balance value, risk, compliance, technical effort, dependencies and learning potential.

04

Measure

Establish indicators such as completion rate, time to onboard, drop-off and manual intervention.
